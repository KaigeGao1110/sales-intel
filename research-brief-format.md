# Research: Sales Brief Generation Format

## Task
Design the prompt/format for generating a 1-page sales brief from company research.

## Focus Areas
1. **Brief Structure** - What sections should the brief have?
2. **Key Questions to Answer** - What problem does this company need solved?
3. **Prompt Engineering** - How to instruct the AI to generate actionable briefs
4. **Data Points to Extract** - What specific signals indicate opportunities?
5. **Output Format** - Markdown? JSON? Plain text?

## Output
Provide:
- Recommended brief template/structure
- Example prompt for generating a brief from raw data
- List of 5-7 "problem indicators" to look for
- Sample brief for a fictional company
